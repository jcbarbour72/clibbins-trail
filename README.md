# The Clibbins Trail

A satirical Oregon Trail prototype: GMT-800s, shade-tree mechanics, and unique ways to die on the way to Oregon City.

**Clibbins** are grass clippings on the road. Slippery. Famous last words: *YEW SLIPD AWN DEM CLIBBINS HOSS.*

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or from this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8765](http://localhost:8765).

Keys: `1–9` choose a menu item, arrows move, `SPACE` / `ENTER` confirm, `S` on the title screen turns on the tiny chiptune. Click menus if you'd rather.

Outfit a trade, a crew, a truck, and a set of jack stands. Buy fuel and taquitos at Matt's. Then try to make the mud hole.

## The joke

Deaths are the payload — 4L60s splitting in second, Harbor Freight jack stands, blown charge pipes, ATF in a Gatorade bottle, Sinclair-taquito dysentery, and the clibbins themselves. Play it like MECC: slow down on grades, don't crawl under a truck on dirt with orange stands, and do not hammer down in the rain in November.

The original pixel death screens in this folder are used in-game.

`?skip` on the URL jumps straight onto the trail with a loaded Sierra, for testing.
